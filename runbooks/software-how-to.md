# Runbook: How-to questions (OneDrive sharing, Teams channels)

**Category:** software · **Owner:** service_desk · **Typical priority:** P4

## Resolution
1. Share the matching knowledge article link from the self-help portal.
2. External OneDrive sharing: the user selects Share, chooses "Specific people", and enters the external address. Links to "Anyone" are blocked by policy.
3. Private Teams channel: the team owner selects the team's menu, then Add channel, then sets Privacy to Private.
4. Offer a 10-minute screen share if the article doesn't solve it.
