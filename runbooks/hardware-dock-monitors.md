# Runbook: Docking station and external monitor problems

**Category:** hardware · **Owner:** desktop_support · **Typical priority:** P3 (the laptop screen is a workaround)

## Resolution
1. Unplug the dock's power for 30 seconds, then reconnect it and the laptop.
2. Check the dock is plugged into the laptop's Thunderbolt or USB-C display port (marked with a lightning or DisplayPort icon).
3. Update the dock firmware and the laptop's graphics driver from the software portal.
4. Flicker: replace the monitor cable and lower the refresh rate to 60 Hz to test.
5. Dock not charging: check the dock's power adapter wattage meets the laptop's requirement.
6. If one monitor works directly but not through the dock, swap in a spare dock.
