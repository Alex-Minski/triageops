# Runbook: Laptop won't power on, black screen, random shutdowns

**Category:** hardware · **Owner:** desktop_support · **Typical priority:** P2 if the user has no other device

## Resolution
1. Confirm the charger's LED is on and try a known-good charger or USB-C port.
2. Hard reset: unplug everything and hold the power button for 30 seconds, then plug in and power on.
3. Black screen after the logo: connect an external monitor. If it displays, the internal panel or its cable has failed.
4. Random shutdowns: check that the vents are clear, then run the vendor's hardware diagnostics from the boot menu.
5. If diagnostics fail or the device is still dead, issue a loaner laptop from stock and open a warranty repair with the vendor.
6. Before handing the device to the vendor, confirm the disk is encrypted, or remove the drive.

## Escalate when
- Swollen battery, burning smell or heat damage: tell the user to stop using it immediately. It is a safety issue.
